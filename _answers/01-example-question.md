## Mukhtasar Jawab

Ji haan. Jab kisi baat ko Qur'an, Hadith ya Islam ki taraf mansoob kiya jaye, to authentic daleel aur durust context dena zaroori hai.

## Ahm Points

1. **Daawa wazeh ho:** Pehle ye samjhein ke asal claim kya hai.
2. **Source verify ho:** Reference mukammal aur checkable ho.
3. **Context dekha jaye:** Aadha quote ya cropped image ghalat tasawwur de sakti hai.
4. **Mukhtalif rai ho to batayein:** Fiqhi ya ilmi ikhtilaf ko imandari se mention karein.

## Behtareen Jawab Ka Tareeqa

- Pehle mukhtasar jawab dein.
- Phir points mein reasoning samjhayen.
- Phir primary source aur trusted explanation dein.
- Aakhir mein nateeja aur adab ke sath baat khatam karein.

## Natija

Maqsad sirf behas jeetna nahi, balkeh baat ko durust, insaf ke sath aur clear evidence ke zariye samjhana hona chahiye.
