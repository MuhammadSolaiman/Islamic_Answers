## Seedha Jawab

Nahi. Screenshot ek supporting item ho sakta hai, lekin final proof tab banta hai jab asal source, date, author aur context verify kiya jaye.

## Screenshot Check Karne Ke Points

1. Kya source ka naam nazar aa raha hai?
2. Kya image edit ya crop ki gayi hai?
3. Kya asal page ya kitab ka reference mojood hai?
4. Kya quote ka pehle aur baad ka context dekha gaya hai?
5. Kya trusted scholar ya reliable source ne is ki tasdeeq ki hai?
