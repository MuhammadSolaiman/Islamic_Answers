## Seedha Jawab

Kisi purani kitab mein kisi waqia ya jumle ka mil jana is baat ki automatic daleel nahi hota ke woh jumla us shakhs ya tamam Sunni ulama ka aqeeda tha. Aqeeda sabit karne ke liye saaf, hosh-o-hawas mein diya gaya, mustaqil aur mu'tabar bayan chahiye hota hai.

## Dost Ko Samjhane Ke Liye Aasan Misaal

> “Yar dekh, agar main Qur'an-e-Pak ki ayat parhoon: **‘Namaz ke qareeb mat jao’**, aur aglay alfaaz **‘jab tum nashe ki halat mein ho’** chhupa doon, to kya ye Qur'an ka kasoor hoga ya mera?
>
> Bilkul isi tarah, Mirza kitab se sirf ‘Chishti Rasool’ wala ilzam uthata hai, magar us se agli line, jahan Ala Hazrat ne isay kufr, shirk aur jahili kaha hai, woh chhupa leta hai. Tum khud batao, ye dhoka hai ya nahi?”

## WhatsApp Par Forward Karne Ke Liye Exact Points

### 1. Sunni Barelvi Ulama Ka Fatwa

Sunni Barelvi ulama ke nazdeek hosh-o-hawas mein **“Chishti Rasool Allah”** kehna shirk aur khula kufr hai.

### 2. Kisi Ka Aqeeda Nahi

Poori dunya mein kisi bhi Sunni ka ye aqeeda nahi hai aur na hi koi isay kalma maanta hai.

### 3. Kitabon Mein Milawat Ka Imkan

Purani kitabon mein ye waqia ya to dushmanon ki taraf se milawat (fabrication) ho sakta hai, ya naqal aur riwayat mein ghalati ka nateeja ho sakta hai. Is liye sirf ek riwayat dekh kar aqeeda sabit nahi hota.

### 4. Khaas Roohani Kaifiyat

Agar ye waqia kisi buzurg se mu'tabar tor par sabit bhi ho, to usay hosh-o-hawas ka aqeeda nahi kaha ja sakta. Ye halat-e-behoshi, sajzb ya majzoobiyat ki kaifiyat ho sakti hai.

### 5. Fatwa Nahi Lagta

Shariat ke usool ke mutabiq aisi halat-e-behoshi mein insan ke munh se nikli baat par usi tarah fatwa nahi lagaya jata jaise hosh mein, iraday ke sath kahi gayi baat par lagta hai.

### 6. Manna Jaiz Nahi

Haalat-e-behoshi ke bawajood woh jumla apni jagah ghalat aur mardood hai. Kisi ke liye bhi usay apna aqeeda banana ya durust samajhna jaiz nahi.

## Jawab Ke Aakhir Mein Dost Se Ye Sawal Poochhein

> “Yar, jab hamare baray ulama khud isay kufr keh rahe hain aur kitabon ki milawat ka imkan bhi bayan kar rahe hain, to Mirza ka ek purani jaali ibarat ko utha kar poore Ahl-e-Sunnat par thopna kahan ka insaf hai?”

## Ahm Usooli Baat

Kisi bhi deeni daaway ko judge karte waqt ye chaar cheezen alag rakhni chahiye:

1. Asal ibarat kya hai?
2. Ibarat ka pehle aur baad ka context kya hai?
3. Riwayat kis sanad ya mu'tabar source se sabit hai?
4. Kya woh shakhs ka mustaqil aqeeda tha ya kisi ghair mamooli halat ka waqia?

## Natija

Ek munqati ya mashkook riwayat ko poore maslak ka aqeeda kehna durust tareeqa nahi. Faisla hamesha mukammal context, mu'tabar source aur wazeh aqeede ki bunyaad par hona chahiye.
